I am a Prospective Graduate Researcher combining a background in Naval Architecture with data-efficient deep learning. My research focuses on developing algorithms for **Automated Visual Inspection (AVI)** and **vision-based Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)**. I am passionate about advancing **Non-Destructive Evaluation (NDE)** and **Condition Assessment** for heavy civil and marine infrastructure where labeled data is limited, expensive, or hard to collect at scale.

My current work develops a novel Interaction-Effect metric and a k–n Fold Augmentation Cross-Validation protocol to systematically improve model performance on small, domain-specific datasets. I applied these to ship hull biofouling detection using a [dataset I collected and curated myself at Narayanganj Dockyard, Bangladesh](/portfolio/03-dataset-collection-narayanganj/). This work is currently in revision following peer review, targeting resubmission.

I completed my undergraduate degree in Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

I am looking to join a lab working on applied computer vision for infrastructure, environmental, or industrial monitoring. I want to address the core challenge of making deep learning work reliably on real, messy, small-scale data rather than large curated benchmarks.

There's a longer story behind how I ended up here, from an electric-vessel design project to teaching myself deep learning to this line of research. [Read my full research statement →](/research-statement/)

## Research Interests
- **Automated Visual Inspection (AVI):** Developing instance segmentation architectures and offline perception pipelines for the in-situ condition assessment of heavy civil and marine infrastructure.
- **Data-Efficient Deep Learning:** Designing systematic augmentation protocols and mathematically optimized cross-validation frameworks to maximize model reliability in extreme data-scarce and visually occluded environments.
- **Non-Contact Infrastructure Assessment:** Translating static vision models to analyze unstructured optical payload data deployed via UAVs, ROVs, and autonomous field platforms for rapid post-event reconnaissance and hazard mitigation.
