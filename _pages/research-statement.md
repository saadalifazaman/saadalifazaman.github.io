My interest in engineering started with math and science olympiads in middle school — the pull was never the recognition, it was the specific feeling of a hard problem finally clicking. That curiosity carried me into Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering at BUET.

The real turn came in my junior year, working with a teammate to design a fully electric 100-passenger vessel for Bangladesh's inland waterways — an unusual direction at a time when electric marine propulsion was barely explored here. The project was eventually published in the *Journal of Maritime Research*, but the more useful outcome was a realization: a vessel's biggest efficiency problem often isn't its engine, it's its hull. Biofouling quietly increases drag, burns more fuel, and shortens maintenance cycles — and almost nobody was detecting it systematically.

That observation redirected my research. I had no formal computer science background, so I taught myself deep learning from scratch — online courses, papers, and long nights running training jobs on Colab and Kaggle. That became my undergraduate thesis: a YOLOv8-based biofouling and corrosion segmentation system, trained on a dataset I collected myself at Narayanganj Dockyard, supported by a RISE Student Research Grant.

Finishing the thesis didn't close the problem, it sharpened it. With only 35 high-resolution images, every result was sensitive to how I ordered and combined data augmentations — a real obstacle for vision systems built for data-scarce industrial settings, not just a footnote. I stayed with that problem after graduating, analyzing 110 pairwise augmentation combinations to build a structured evaluation framework, now a manuscript under peer review.

What I'm chasing next is the layer above perception. A model that can spot biofouling or corrosion still needs to be paired with the sensing, control, and decision-making that let a physical system act on what it sees. That's the direction I want my graduate research to take — from vision systems that classify defects toward complete inspection and monitoring platforms for maritime, infrastructure, and environmental applications.
