**Role:** Junior Year Project (May 2022 – May 2023)  
**Supervisor:** Dr. Md. Mashiur Rahaman, Professor, Dept. of Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering, BUET

---

**The problem:** Bangladesh's inland waterway transport network relies heavily on diesel-powered vessels, contributing significantly to CO₂ emissions and fuel costs along high-density passenger routes.

**What I designed:** A conceptual 100-passenger inland vessel with a fully electric propulsion system tailored to the operational constraints of Bangladesh's inland waterways — route lengths, load profiles, and charging infrastructure availability. The design was benchmarked against a comparable conventional diesel vessel across energy efficiency, emissions output, and operational cost metrics.

**Outcome:** The electric propulsion concept demonstrated meaningful reductions in CO₂ emissions and long-term operational costs compared to the diesel baseline, contributing to the published paper in the *Journal of Maritime Research* (2023).

[Published Paper — Journal of Maritime Research, 20(3), 68–71](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/376992611)
