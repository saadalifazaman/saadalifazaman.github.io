To mitigate small-data bias in industrial computer vision, this work proposes a k–n Fold Augmentation Cross-Validation (k–n Fold ACV) protocol together with a novel Interaction-Effect (IE) metric that quantifies order-sensitivity among data augmentations. Validated on a newly curated dataset (NDBD) of 35 high-resolution ship-hull images across YOLOv8m-seg, YOLO11m-seg, and Mask R-CNN. [Code and dataset](https://github.com/saadalifazaman/NDBD) and [Preprint (SSRN)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5966947) are publicly available.
